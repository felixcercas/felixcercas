## 🇺🇸 English

### About me

Bilingual commercial-finance professional with a master's in Economic Development and Climate Change (CATIE). I pair day-to-day financial operations with data and AI tools to make processes clearer, faster and measurable.

- I handle accounts, vendor partnerships, UCC filings and compliance at **[Multipli](https://github.com/Multipli-git)**, a commercial financing and leasing company operating in the US, Canada and Mexico.
- I build **BI dashboards, financial reports and automations** in Zoho (CRM, Desk, Analytics) for the team.
